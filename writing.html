The Quietest Rebellion · 2025

The things I couldn't say out loud, I learned to write down instead.

I wrote this book in Class 11 while trying to understand myself and exploring solitude. It began as notes from self-reflection, not as a plan to publish a book.

Buy the book ↗Back to the life story

Eventually the manuscript became a challenge: could I take something deeply private, learn the publishing process by myself, and make it public at zero cost?

45 poems · six movements · ISBN 9789334327304

A notebook became a book.

Writing down what I learned and what I felt made room for new thoughts and insights to come in.

I did not begin with the intention of becoming an author. The book grew because reflection itself became a habit. Then I decided to take the work public, learn the publishing process, handle the ISBN and the rest myself, and prove that I could take an internal practice all the way to a finished artifact.

The title page beside this story is deliberately simple: The Quietest Rebellion, written by Kaushik Pratap Singh. It gives the book a visible identity in the repository and on the website even when the original raster cover asset is unavailable.

The cards below are not meant to replace the poems. They are my interpretations of what I think each poem is trying to say, so a visitor can understand the world of the book before deciding whether to enter it.
